# キャラクター素材

`public/assets/companion.png` は、このアプリ用に組み込みのimage_genツールで生成したオリジナル素材です。RGBA PNG、1145×1374、背景透過。1回の生成で作成し、組み込み前に外観とアルファチャンネルを確認しています。

最終生成プロンプト：

> Use case: stylized-concept
> Asset type: compact 420px Japanese AI-DLC workflow companion web app side-panel character asset
> Primary request: one original adorable small round lavender workshop creature, front-facing full body, holding a tiny blank notebook, wearing a small pale yellow architect cap
> Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent background with no scenery or ground
> Subject: a single centered compact round lavender creature with a clean readable silhouette, dark simple oval eyes, tiny neutral smile, small arms holding the blank notebook, small feet, small pale yellow architect cap
> Style/medium: clean friendly hand-painted clay-like 2D illustration, soft subtle painted texture, polished UI companion mascot, crisp enough to remain recognizable at 72px
> Composition/framing: front-facing full body, centered, generous transparent padding on all sides, no cropping, upright pose
> Lighting/mood: soft even studio-like shading, calm friendly workshop feeling
> Color palette: lavender body, pale yellow cap, muted cream notebook, very dark eyes and outline accents
> Materials/textures: gentle hand-painted/clay-like surface texture without photorealism
> Text (verbatim): none; notebook must be completely blank
> Constraints: exactly one character and one blank notebook; preserve actual alpha transparency; simple silhouette; no text, no logo, no scenery, no props beyond the cap and notebook
> Avoid: extra characters, animals, tools, desk, background color, white matte, checkerboard baked into the image, shadows outside the character, lettering, symbols, watermark, cropped body, side or three-quarter view, exaggerated expression, busy detail
